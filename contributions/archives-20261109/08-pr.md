# Preserve GNU tar member identities at the long-name limit

Source: [de2c63908909cab675887103921ed2601b1bae9b](https://github.com/Afloat16/craft-anything/commit/de2c63908909cab675887103921ed2601b1bae9b)

Upstream PR: [#1362](https://github.com/storytold/craft-anything/pull/1362)

## Problem
GNU LongName and LongLink metadata was cut to 4096 bytes before conversion to text. A longer member name or link target therefore appeared under a different, truncated identity, which could collide with a real shorter member.

## Change
Inspect the declared metadata without copying past the name budget. Accept complete identities at the limit, including size-delimited names and early NUL termination. Stop with a warning on an oversized identity or incomplete extension body, preserving earlier members.

## Validation
