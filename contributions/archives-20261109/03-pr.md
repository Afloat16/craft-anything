# Validate gzip header checksums without rejecting informational fields

Source: [dde4aa0233be968f9b55bd3316204b880b8dbf2e](https://github.com/Afloat16/craft-anything/commit/dde4aa0233be968f9b55bd3316204b880b8dbf2e)

Upstream PR: [#1371](https://github.com/storytold/craft-anything/pull/1371)

## Problem
Gzip headers with an FHCRC were accepted even when the header checksum was wrong, while otherwise readable streams were rejected solely for informational XFL or OS values.

## Change
Check the low 16 bits of the header CRC-32 when FHCRC is present. Accept informational encoder bytes and preserve checks for the compression method, reserved flag bits, optional-field boundaries and trailer integrity.

The framing follows [RFC 1952 section 2.3.1](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc1952#section-2.3.1).
